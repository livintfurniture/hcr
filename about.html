About HCR Hyderabad Cab Rentals

Ten years of dependable journeys, one trip at a time.

Our Story

Built in Hyderabad, Trusted Across South India

HCR Hyderabad Cab Rentals began in Hyderabad as part of World Tours and Travels, backed by more than 10 years of experience in the travel business. Our promise has always been simple: show up on time, drive safely, and treat every passenger like family. Today, that promise has grown into a fleet of over 100 cabs serving families, students, corporates, and pilgrims across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu.

Whether it's an early-morning airport transfer, a family pilgrimage trip, or a multi-city outstation drive, our team designs the journey around you — not the other way around.
