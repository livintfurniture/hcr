Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

HCR Hyderabad Cab Rentals ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you submit a booking enquiry or contact form, we collect the details you provide — name, phone number, email address, pickup/destination locations, travel dates, and any message you enter. We do not collect payment information through this website.

How We Use Your Information

Enquiry details are used solely to respond to your travel request, confirm bookings, and provide customer support via phone, WhatsApp, or email. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

WhatsApp Communication

Our booking and contact forms open a pre-filled WhatsApp message on your device. Sending that message is your choice — it is transmitted directly via WhatsApp, not stored on our servers.

Cookies

We may use Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion measurement. These set cookies and collect anonymous usage data — such as pages viewed and whether a call or WhatsApp button was tapped — to help us improve the website and measure our advertising. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or opt out through Google's Ads Settings. Standard browser caching may also be used to improve page load performance.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Maps to display our office location, Google Fonts for typography, and Google Analytics / Google Ads for measurement. These services may collect standard technical data (such as IP address) as governed by their own privacy policies.

Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, email us at info@hyderabadcabrentals.com or call +91 95800 12345.
